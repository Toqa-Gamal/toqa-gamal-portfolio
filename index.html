Testing & Verification

What I enjoy most about Computer Engineering is not only building a system — it's checking whether it actually behaves the way it was supposed to.

I discovered that I naturally enjoy the testing side of engineering: reading requirements, creating scenarios, exploring edge cases, finding bugs, and then asking why that bug happened.

Attention to Detail

I naturally pay attention to the small things that can easily be overlooked.

In testing, that means looking beyond the happy path, checking boundaries, exploring unexpected scenarios, and noticing things that don't quite fit.

For me, a detail is not just a small part of the system — it can be the clue that explains a much bigger problem.

Fashion & Design

I find a similar mindset in something else I genuinely love: fashion and design.

I enjoy exploring designs and paying attention to details, patterns, proportions, materials, and how every element fits together.

Whether I'm looking at a system or a design, I enjoy understanding why every part is there and whether everything fits together.

My Engineering Mindset

For me, verification is more than saying "it works."

It means checking that the system matches its requirements and continues to behave correctly when the scenario changes.

That is the mindset I want to bring to every project: understand, question, investigate, and verify.
